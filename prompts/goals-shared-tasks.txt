## 2. Background
The branch adds an opt-in SwiftUI/Liquid Glass presentation behind the compile flag OMI_IOS_SWIFTUI (Dart const iosSwiftUiEnabled; native only when the flag is on AND Platform.isIOS AND supportsIosSwiftUi()). Flutter screens project their state into versioned snapshots of rows (IosNativeSurface, NativeRow, NativeSection, showIosNativeModal, showOmiSheet nativeBuilder, IosNativeEdit, ...) that a SwiftUI renderer draws; callbacks stay in Dart with the existing owners; any invalid row makes the whole screen fall back to the complete Flutter screen.
