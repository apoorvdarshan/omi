P8 defines a level row's subtitle as the short, localized current-value label ('75%', 'Level 3'). Swift draws it on the title line as `Text(valueLabel).monospacedDigit().foregroundStyle(.secondary).fixedSize()` (app/ios/Runner/NativeUI/NativeSurfaceView.swift:1143-1147), so the text cannot wrap.
