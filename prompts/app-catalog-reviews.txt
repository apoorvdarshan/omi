## 2. Background
The branch adds an opt-in SwiftUI/Liquid Glass presentation behind the compile flag OMI_IOS_SWIFTUI (Dart const iosSwiftUiEnabled; native only when the flag is on AND Platform.isIOS AND supportsIosSwiftUi()).
